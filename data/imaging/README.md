# Imaging for the sample family

Five openly licensed medical images from Wikimedia Commons. `python -m tools.family` places them in imaging reports from fictional radiology centres. Each report prints the image's credit and licence under the image, as CC BY and CC BY-SA require. Nabz stores imaging reports as "other records" and does not analyse them.
